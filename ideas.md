# fourtee2 Universe — Reference-Led Design Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference

The supplied reference image is the non-negotiable visual specification for this rebuild. It establishes a **black, cinematic, cosmological** canvas anchored by a deep-space hero, a large white fourtee2 wordmark, sparse monospaced supporting copy, and three editorial portal cards. The experience should feel like an archival portal into a brand universe rather than a conventional marketing landing page.

## Chosen Approach: Astral Editorial System

**Design Movement:** Late-1990s/Y2K digital editorial merged with contemporary astronomy photography. The interface is intentionally restrained and distant, allowing cosmic imagery and typography to carry the atmosphere.

**Core Principles:** Near-black negative space creates gravity; sparse content is arranged in a precise vertical rhythm; typography is treated as brand architecture; images are cropped as cinematic portals rather than generic content cards.

**Color Philosophy:** Absolute black is the operating field, with midnight cobalt, diffuse violet, and rare warm city-light gold appearing only inside image worlds. White is reserved for the most important wordmarks and operational labels; muted graphite keeps the interface understated.

**Layout Paradigm:** A single, slow vertical telescope: a compact top rail, an oversized cosmological viewing field, a deliberately empty transit zone, then three offset universe portals. The page avoids a conventional centered content grid by using asymmetric card heights and measured dead space.

**Signature Elements:** Hairline rules, deliberately tracked monospaced captions, and a low-opacity star field act as the recurring connective tissue. Image wells receive an inky bottom fade so wordmarks feel embedded in the image rather than placed over it.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interaction is quiet and archival. Navigation and cards reveal themselves with a slight lift, a thin rule, and a controlled image-scale shift; there are no animated buttons or decorative effects competing with the universe.

**Animation:** Content fades upward by a few pixels on first reveal, with transitions under 300ms and a strong ease-out. Image portals drift less than 3% on hover. All nonessential motion is removed for users who prefer reduced motion.

**Typography System:** Righteous is exclusively reserved for every occurrence of fourtee2 and its wordmark extensions. IBM Plex Mono supplies spaced operational labels and navigation, while a neutral system sans is used only where compact supporting copy improves legibility.

**Brand Essence:** A cosmic index for people who choose their world, their sound, and their ritual with intent. **Vast, precise, nocturnal.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are minimal, declarative, and spatial; CTAs read like navigational commands rather than sales prompts. Example: “Explore the universe.” Example: “Choose your frequency.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** The fourtee2 wordmark is the primary signature and is always set in Righteous. Its thick, rounded geometry contrasts with the small, engineered mono labels and remains the page’s brightest visual anchor.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Event Horizon Black** — `#030405` — establishes an ownable field that lets the universe, not the interface, emit light.
